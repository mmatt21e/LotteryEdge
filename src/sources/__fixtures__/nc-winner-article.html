Iredell County man pockets $200,000 top prize

Tuesday, July 14, 2026 By: NCEL
Tweet
NCEL graphic

Ray White of Statesville tried his luck on a $5 scratch-off and won a $200,000 top prize.

White bought his 5 Times Lucky ticket from Speedy Gas on Salisbury Road in Statesville.

He arrived at lottery headquarters Monday to claim his prize and, after required state and federal tax withholdings, took home $144,021.

The 5 Times Lucky game debuted in July 2025 with five $200,000 top prizes. One $200,000 prize remains to be claimed.

Education programs in Iredell County received $10.9 million in support from lottery funds last year. For details on how lottery funds have made a difference in all of North Carolina’s 100 counties, visit www.nclottery.com and click on the “Impact” section of the lottery’s website.
